## 1. Vision (one paragraph)

A map-first mobile app for discovering food. Restaurants and home cooks appear on a map as **country-flag markers** for their cuisine. Users browse menus, send **pickup requests** and **table requests** (like a phone call, but in the app), and keep a personal **food log** of photos that fills a GitHub-style **activity grid** on their profile. Sellers can pay to turn their flag into a gently animated **ad marker**.

Launch target: one city, ~100 restaurants, ~1,000 users.
**Launch city:** TBD (Ontario, Canada)

## 4. Navigation

- Current (built through spec 11): a signed-in user has **Restaurants · Homemade · Requests · Profile**; with seller mode on the tab bar is **Requests · Manage · Profile** (the two map tabs are hidden; Manage's screen is titled **Manage Business**). Guests see **Restaurants · Homemade · Profile** only. Requests shows the person's sent requests; a business owner (whatever the seller mode) gets **Received** and **Sent** switches, opening on Received with seller mode on and on Sent with it off. The Requests tab number is, for an owner, the requests waiting for them plus own requests that changed; for a customer, own requests that changed since they last opened Sent.
- **Final tab bar plan** (reached by specs 10b and 16; Requests is built, Offers is still to come):
  - Seller mode off: **Restaurants · Homemade · Offers · Requests · Profile**
  - Seller mode on: **Requests · Manage · Profile** (the maps and Offers are hidden in seller mode)
- Guests (signed out) can browse the Restaurants and Homemade tabs. Signing in is required for the profile, seller mode and requests; the guest Profile tab shows a "Sign in / Create account" prompt.
- One account for everything. Seller mode is a switch on the same account, not a separate app or login.

## 5. Sellers & listings

### Onboarding
1. User turns on seller mode and picks a type: **Restaurant** or **Homemade**.
2. Enters business name and **address via map-API autocomplete**. The selected address must resolve to a real place; latitude/longitude are filled automatically. No manual pin dropping.
3. Picks their **cuisine/country** from a pre-populated list. That country's flag becomes their marker.
4. Can change cuisine, details, and menu any time from Manage Business.

### Menus
- A seller can either add **itemized menu items** (name, optional description, price, photo, available yes/no) **or** just upload **menu photos**. Both are valid.
- Because the seller accepts or declines every request, a free-text order is acceptable when only menu photos exist.

### Homemade specifics
- On the map, homemade sellers show at an **approximate location** (rounded / offset area, not exact).
- The **exact address** is shown to a customer only after the seller **accepts** their request.
- Every homemade listing shows a **food-safety & allergy disclaimer**.

## 6. Map

- Full-screen map (react-native-maps). Both Restaurants and Homemade tabs use it, each showing only its own sellers.
- **Organic marker:** compact country flag.
- **Clustering:** nearby markers group when zoomed out.
- Tapping a marker opens a **draggable bottom sheet** (collapsed / half / full). The map stays where it was.
- Bottom sheet, menu first (spec 08 Decision 15): name and cuisine flag in a fixed header; a short cover banner; (homemade) a small "Food safety and allergies" row that opens its full text on a tap; menu-page photos (small, tap to enlarge); menu items with prices/availability; gallery; and at the bottom the address with **Directions** (restaurant) or an "approximate area only" note (homemade). **Request pickup**, **Request table** (restaurants that allow it) and **Report** arrive with specs 09, 11 and 17.
- If a business can't be shown as a flag (no country fits), use a neutral cuisine icon.

## 7. Ads (promoted markers)

- A promoted seller's marker is still a **small flag**, but it **rises and falls smoothly in a loop** (subtle, eased, no bouncing or flashing).
- Respect the OS **reduce-motion** setting: no animation, keep a static highlight ring instead.
- **Clustered / zoomed out:** when a promoted marker is inside a cluster, it is pulled out and shown on top of the cluster with a highlight, still animating.
- **Zoomed in:** normal markers behave normally; the promoted one keeps its rise-and-fall.
- Shows a small **"Ad"** badge on the marker, and **"Sponsored"** in the bottom sheet when tapped.
- Campaign = promoted seller + start date + end date + status. **Fixed price per campaign.**
- No cap on number of ads in v1.
- **Open question:** how campaigns are paid in v1 (manual invoice + admin activation, or in-app payment). Default until decided: admin activates campaigns manually.

## 8. Pickup requests

Flow: pick seller → choose items (or write a free-text order) → choose pickup time → submit → seller accepts/declines → customer sees status.

Statuses: `pending` → `accepted` | `declined` | `expired` | `cancelled` (by customer while pending) → `completed` / `no_show` (marked by seller after pickup time).

Rules:
- A pending request is never shown to the customer as confirmed.
- Requests not answered by the seller within a set window auto-**expire** (default 30 min, or at pickup time, whichever is first).
- Duplicate submit protection (idempotency key).
- No payment in app. Terms of service explain the trust-based model.
- Limits: a customer can have up to 3 waiting pickup requests and 1 waiting request per place. There is no limit on how many requests they send over time, except that at most 10 requests per place in any rolling 24 hours are accepted (answered, cancelled and expired ones count toward the 10), which stops a script from flooding one inbox.

## 9. Table requests

Flow: date, time, party size, optional note → submit → seller accepts/declines.

Same status model as pickup. Restaurants choose whether they accept table requests.

Limits: up to 3 waiting table requests per customer and 1 waiting per restaurant, and at most 10 table requests per restaurant in any rolling 24 hours (any status counts). They are counted apart from pickup requests: waiting pickups never block a table request, and the reverse.

**I've arrived:** on an accepted table request, from 2 hours before the table time until 4 hours after it (by the server clock), the customer can tap "I've arrived" once. This sends a one-time notification to the seller. No location tracking.

## 10. Reports

- A customer can **report a seller** (reason + optional note), e.g. declined after acceptance, unsafe food, wrong info, no-show by seller.
- Reports go to an admin review queue.
- Once an admin **confirms** a report, it is visible on the seller's public profile (e.g. "1 confirmed report").
- Sellers can also mark customers as `no_show` on requests. (Customer-facing display of this is an open question.)

## 11. Profile, food log & activity grid

### Profile
Photo, display name, username, bio, city. Seller users show a link to their business.
Reserved usernames that nobody can take (they could pass as staff): admin, administrator, support, help, staff, moderator, system, root, liketoorder. Enforced in the database and in the app form.

### Food log uploads
- **One photo per upload**, with a short description and a **required country/cuisine** (same list as map flags).
- Timestamp is set by the server at upload. **No backdating.**
- Each upload is **public or private** (public by default).
- Each upload has its own detail page: full image, full description, country, date. This page becomes the community post page in a later version.

### Activity grid — two views (toggle)
**Activity view (default):** each square = one day, color strength = number of uploads.
- 0 = empty · 1 = light · 2–3 = medium · 4+ = dark (warm food color)

**Cuisine view:**
- All of that day's uploads are the same country → that country's **flag**
- Uploads from different countries → **🌍**
- No uploads → empty

**Shared rules:**
- Day = the user's **local date at upload time** (stored with the upload). Avoids late-night uploads landing on the next day.
- Mobile shows the **last ~4 months** with a "View full year" option.
- Tap a day → panel listing that day's uploads: thumbnail, flag, one-line trimmed description. Empty day shows "Nothing logged on <date>".
- Tap an upload → its detail page.
- Every square has an accessible label, e.g. "Oct 12 – 3 uploads".
- Stats above the grid: total this year, current streak, longest streak, cuisines tried.
- Private uploads count on the owner's own grid only; visitors don't see them.
- Sellers have the same personal grid.

A seller who declines must pick one of six reasons (Other needs their own words); a seller who accepts may add a note (a homemade cook is told not to type their address). The customer sees "Declined: <reason>" and "Seller's note: <note>" in the list and in the request detail, and a number on the Requests tab for requests that changed since they last opened Sent (kept on the phone, per user). The manual run was the iOS Simulator only, run by Archan (not a real iPhone or Android); a real iPhone, Android, VoiceOver and TalkBack, and the dialog keyboard offset on a device stay in section 16 as before-launch items, with the notes that the badge is replaced by spec 12, `MyRequestsScreen` is now unused, two cross-feature imports are to be tidied, and the customer request detail test is flaky under load. No native module was added: no rebuild is needed.
- 2026-11-10 — Spec 11 shipped (PR 1 database, PR 2 app): a signed-in customer can open a restaurant that accepts tables, tap **Request table**, pick a day and a time (always shown with its zone name, for example 7:00 pm EST), a party of 1 to 20 and an optional note, and follow it in **Sent** next to pickup requests; the restaurant owner sees it in **Received** (Waiting / All), accepts it (with an optional note) or declines it with one of five table reasons (Other needs their own words), and can mark it Completed or No-show after the table time. On an accepted request the customer taps **I've arrived** once, from 2 hours before the table time to 4 hours after it by the server clock, and the restaurant sees "Customer has arrived". Every row speaks its kind first, the two copies of paged-list code became one shared hook, and Sent moved to the same `(created_at, id)` cursor as the inbox. The manual run was on the iOS Simulator only, run by Archan (not a real iPhone, not Android). Archan's requested changes became planned rows 11b request-limits, 11c requests-filters and 11d theme-refresh.
- 2026-11-11 - Spec 11b shipped (one PR, two gates): the hourly request limit is gone. A customer can have up to 3 waiting pickup requests and 3 waiting table requests, and 1 waiting per place, and can keep asking the same place over time once the earlier request is answered, cancelled or expired. A backstop refuses the 11th request by one customer to one place (per kind) in a rolling 24 hours, any status counting, so a submit-and-cancel loop cannot flood one inbox. Two limit messages were reworded ("1 per place"; "try again tomorrow"). The local seed has a customer (seed-customer-limit) already over the limit at one restaurant. No native module, app rebuild or type change. Manual check: iOS Simulator only, run by Archan, not a real iPhone or Android. Carry-forward: an overall daily cap across places and notification throttling (spec 12).
